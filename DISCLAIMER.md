# Legal & Medical Disclaimer
## हमारे नुस्खे / Hamare Nuskhe

---

### 1. Non-Medical Archival Purpose

The content published in **हमारे नुस्खे (Hamare Nuskhe)** is compiled and archived solely for **cultural preservation, educational inquiry, and ethnobotanical documentation**. 

**IT IS NOT MEDICAL ADVICE, DIAGNOSIS, OR TREATMENT.**

The practices described in this archive reflect domestic household traditions, oral folklore, and classical literature. Neither the authors, curators, reviewers, nor contributors warrant the therapeutic efficacy or clinical safety of any recipe.

---

### 2. Clinical Consultation Warning

- Always consult a qualified, registered medical practitioner (MBBS / MD or registered AYUSH practitioner) prior to consuming or applying any botanical preparation.
- This is particularly critical if you are:
  - Pregnant or attempting to conceive
  - Breastfeeding
  - Elderly or immunocompromised
  - Managing a chronic illness (e.g. hypertension, diabetes, kidney or liver disease)
  - Scheduled for surgery or dental operations
  - Currently taking any prescription medications (including blood thinners, blood pressure agents, or oral hypoglycemics)

---

### 3. Emergency & Red-Flag Symptoms

Home remedies must **never** be used to delay urgent medical evaluation. Seek immediate emergency care at a hospital if you or a family member experience:
- Persistent or high fever (above 101°F / 38.3°C)
- Shortness of breath, chest pain, or laboured respiration
- Blood in phlegm, saliva, vomit, stool, or urine
- Sudden, severe, or unrelenting abdominal pain
- Severe dehydration, dizziness, fainting, or confusion
- Signs of jaundice (yellowing of skin or eyes)

---
