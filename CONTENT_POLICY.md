# Content Policy & Editorial Standards
## हमारे नुस्खे / Hamare Nuskhe

---

## 1. What is Strictly Excluded

To protect human health and maintain scientific integrity, the following categories are **strictly banned** from this archive:

1. **Remedies for Serious / Life-Threatening Diseases:**
   - Any remedy claimed to &quot;cure&quot;, &quot;treat&quot;, or replace medical therapy for cancer, tuberculosis, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, chronic kidney or liver disease, jaundice, epilepsy, or mental illness.
2. **Pediatric Dosing & Infant Care:**
   - No preparations meant for newborns or infants under 1 year of age.
3. **Toxic, Corrosive, or Dangerous Plants:**
   - Unprocessed *Aconitum* (vatsanabha), raw *Gloriosa superba* (kalihari), unprocessed *Strychnos nux-vomica* (kupilu), *Datura*, or toxic euphorbias.
   - Caustic or burn-inducing topical concoctions (e.g., undiluted bhut jolokia pepper rubs).
4. **Animal-Derived or Raw Meat Products:**
   - Blood, organs, or uncooked meats.
5. **Magic, Occult, or Supernatural Claims:**
   - Astrological or ritualistic amulets and incantations.

---

## 2. Mandatory Sourcing Rules

- Every remedy must cite at least one **real, documented source**:
  - AYUSH / CCRAS / CSIR pharmacopoeias and research monographs
  - Peer-reviewed ethnobotany journals (e.g. *Indian Journal of Traditional Knowledge*)
  - Regional university botanical surveys
  - Regional cookbooks with documented culinary history
  - Documented oral history interviews with named community elders
- **Zero Fabrication:** Never invent a botanical binomial, citation, dosage, or folklore attribution. If an attribute is unknown, set `null` and mark `needsExpertReview: true`.

---

## 3. Native Script Standards

- Regional titles must be rendered in the native script of the originating state (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu).
- Unicode blocks are strictly verified in our seed validator. Guesswork or pseudo-phonetic transliterations are prohibited.

---

## 4. Consent & Privacy (DPDP Act 2023)

- Living family elders or community knowledge holders must give explicit consent before their names or community practices are published.
- User contributions do not store IP addresses or tracking cookies.
